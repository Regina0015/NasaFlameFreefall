# Texture sources

## earth-surface-2k.jpg

- Content: equirectangular Earth map, 2048 x 1024, land, ocean, ice and clouds in one image.
  The clouds are baked into the image, so the scene adds no separate cloud layer.
- Source: NASA Scientific Visualization Studio, "Equirectangular Projected Earth for LARGEST" (SVS 3615).
- Page: https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/3615
- File: https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/vis/a000000/a003600/a003615/flat_earth03.jpg
- Credit: Greg Shirah (NASA/GSFC SVS). Data from Terra and Aqua MODIS, Blue Marble: Next Generation
  (courtesy of Reto Stockli, NASA Earth Observatory) and CPC cloud composites.
- License: NASA media are generally not copyrighted (public domain); see https://www.nasa.gov/nasa-brand-center/images-and-media/
- Downloaded: 2026-11-15, unmodified.

## northern-mexico-regional.jpg

- Content: regional satellite-style imagery of northern Mexico and south Texas (BlueMarble Shaded Relief + Bathymetry),
  EPSG:4326 (plain latitude/longitude), 2400 x 2000 px, longitude -107 to -95, latitude 21 to 31 (0.005 degrees per pixel,
  about 550 m). Used only for the opening of the login animation, as a patch on the globe. It is not live imagery.
- Source: NASA GIBS (Global Imagery Browse Services), layer BlueMarble_ShadedRelief_Bathymetry, from NASA Earth Observatory's
  Blue Marble. WMS GetMap: https://gibs.earthdata.nasa.gov/wms/epsg4326/best/wms.cgi (BBOX=-107,21,-95,31).
- License: NASA imagery is generally not copyrighted; see https://www.earthdata.nasa.gov/engage/open-data-services-software/data-information-policy
- Downloaded: 2026-11-15, unmodified (no key or account needed).
