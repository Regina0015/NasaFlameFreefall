# Model sources

## iss-b.glb  (primary, used in the login intro)
- Copied unmodified from `International Space Station (ISS) (B).glb`.
- Model: International Space Station (ISS) (B). Credit: NASA / Michael D. Carbajal.
- Draco-compressed glTF. The decoder is served from `public/draco/` (copied from three.js `examples/jsm/libs/draco/gltf`, MIT license).

## iss-a.glb  (alternative, not used)
- Copied unmodified from `International Space Station (ISS) (A).glb`. Low-poly alternative.
- Author credit not recorded here. Confirm it on the NASA 3D Resources page before using this model.

## twisters-station-solid.glb, twisters-station-hologram.glb
- Procedural conceptual models generated by `crear-modelos.cjs`. Kept for the dashboard shell.

## flametrace-habitat-concept.glb
- Original conceptual habitat made for FlameTrace: a central passage, a research lab, crew quarters and a stowage bay. It is not an ISS replica and not the inside of the NASA model.
- Generated, not hand-edited. Source: `src/lib/habitat/` (`layout.js` spatial layout, `build.js` geometry, `metadata.js` names). Rebuild with `npm run export:habitat` (script: `tools/export-habitat.mjs`).
- The viewer builds the same geometry at runtime; the GLB is the reusable export of it.
